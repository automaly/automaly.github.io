How it works

From first call to a running service.

Five stages, each ending in something you can see and keep. Click a stage, or press play for the guided tour.

  1. Discover

    A free 30-minute call. We listen, ask awkward questions and tell you honestly whether we are the right fit.

    Day 1

  2. Shape

    A written specification, a clickable prototype and a fixed quote. You approve the scope before any code is written.

    1–2 weeks

  3. Build

    Two-week sprints with a live staging link. You see progress every week and can steer at any time.

    4–10 weeks

  4. Launch

    Testing, data migration, domain and email set-up, then a calm go-live with us on hand.

    1 week

  5. Run & grow

    Monitoring, backups, updates and a roadmap. We stay on after launch for as long as you need us.
